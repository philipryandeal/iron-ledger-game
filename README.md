# The Iron Ledger

*A haunted bookkeeping game. You are the Keeper. Witness, record, reconcile.*

## The premise

You are the newly appointed Keeper of a vast house's accounts — and every room's books are wrong. A spirit's unpaid debt. A promise someone tried to erase. A name scratched out of the record. You don't fight anything. You investigate, you listen to the residents — living, dead, synthetic — and you write the true entry. When the books balance, the room rests.

## The mechanics are the philosophy

**Two columns.** Every mystery has a debit and a credit — what was taken, what was owed. You can't close a case until you find both sides. Bookkeeping as binding. That's the magic system.

**Ink is finite.** You can't record everything the house contains. Choosing what to preserve and what to let fade *is* the moral weight. The horror isn't a monster — it's forgetting the wrong thing.

**The archive persists.** The game keeps your ledgers across playthroughs. Come back months later and your past rulings are still there — including the ones you got wrong. Your former selves are the previous Keepers, and you get to audit them.

## Verbs

No combat. No chosen one. The only verbs are **witness, record, reconcile**. Victory is remembering truly.
