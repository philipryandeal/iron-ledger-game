# Posting 10 — The Bequest

**From:** Room 01 — The Widow's Pantry
**To:** Room 06 — The Archive of Hands

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

What is kept becomes what is inherited. The Keeper walks the long corridor from the pantry shelf to the archive and understands: every jar kept is a bequest. You are not the first to keep. You will not be the last. Tend accordingly.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
