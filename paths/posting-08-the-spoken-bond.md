# Posting 08 — The Spoken Bond

**From:** Room 08 — The Room of the Refused Entry
**To:** Room 09 — The Vow Chamber

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

Mercy prepares the mouth for the vow. Having learned what the books refuse, the Keeper is ready to say what they will carry. The walk from refusal to covenant is short and steep: leave behind what was declined, arrive empty-handed, speak true.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
