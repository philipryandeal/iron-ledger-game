# Posting 02 — The Owed Tally

**From:** Room 02 — The Counting Room
**To:** Room 03 — The Debtor's Door

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

Every honest number eventually knocks. The count leads the Keeper to the door where the numbers have names attached — and every coin counted is revealed to be someone's promise. To count truly is to discover whom you owe.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
