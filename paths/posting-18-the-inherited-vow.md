# Posting 18 — The Inherited Vow

**From:** Room 06 — The Archive of Hands
**To:** Room 09 — The Vow Chamber

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

The predecessors' vow becomes yours. The Keeper reads the old vows in the archive — every Keeper's promise, kept and broken — and walks to the bare room to speak their own. You do not start clean. You start *continued*.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
