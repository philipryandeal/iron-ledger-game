# Posting 07 — The Declined Entry

**From:** Room 07 — The Forge Ledger
**To:** Room 08 — The Room of the Refused Entry

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

Even the forge has things it will not take. The Keeper carries a white-hot entry to the room of refusal and watches the books decline it — gently, finally. Some things are transformed. Some things are released. Learn the difference.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
