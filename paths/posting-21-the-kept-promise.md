# Posting 21 — The Kept Promise

**From:** Room 01 — The Widow's Pantry
**To:** Room 09 — The Vow Chamber

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

The longest vow in the house: from the first keeping to the final promise. The Keeper carries the bay leaf from the pantry — *before the case, the bay leaf* — all the way to the vow chamber, and understands that keeping *was* the vow, all along.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
