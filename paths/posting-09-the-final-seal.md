# Posting 09 — The Final Seal

**From:** Room 09 — The Vow Chamber
**To:** Room 10 — The Closing Entry

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

The vow spoken becomes the seal pressed. The Keeper walks the last path carrying nothing but the promise — and finds that the closing entry was already written, long ago, waiting for someone worthy to post it. Sign it. Mean it. Walk out.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
