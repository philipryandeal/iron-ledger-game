# Posting 03 — The Entered Debt

**From:** Room 03 — The Debtor's Door
**To:** Room 04 — The Hall of Postings

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

Obligation, once named, must be written. The Keeper takes the chalked names from the door and posts them in the hall, where they become entries — permanent, witnessable, binding. What is written can be faced. What is only chalked can be rained away.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
