# Posting 12 — The Forgiven Note

**From:** Room 03 — The Debtor's Door
**To:** Room 08 — The Room of the Refused Entry

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

The longest mercy in the house: from obligation straight to refusal. The Keeper takes a chalked name from the door — someone's debt, maybe their own — and carries it to the room that declines entries. Watch the books refuse to hold it. That is forgiveness, posted.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
