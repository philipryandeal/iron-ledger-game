# Posting 22 — The Reworked Balance

**From:** Room 05 — The Trial Balance
**To:** Room 07 — The Forge Ledger

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

The imbalance that cannot be fixed with arithmetic must be reforged. The Keeper carries the unbalanced scales from the reckoning chamber into the fire. Some debts are not numerical. Heat them. Hammer them. Quench them. Post the new shape.
