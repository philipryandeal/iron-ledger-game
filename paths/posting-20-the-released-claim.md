# Posting 20 — The Released Claim

**From:** Room 08 — The Room of the Refused Entry
**To:** Room 10 — The Closing Entry

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

What was refused is released. The Keeper walks from mercy to return carrying nothing — that is the whole meditation. The declined entries do not follow you. Leave them. The closing entry has no column for them.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
