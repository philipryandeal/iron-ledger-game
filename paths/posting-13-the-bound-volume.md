# Posting 13 — The Bound Volume

**From:** Room 04 — The Hall of Postings
**To:** Room 06 — The Archive of Hands

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

The record, bound, joins the inheritance. The Keeper gathers the hall's postings into a volume and shelves it among the predecessors' ledgers. Your entries are now someone's archive. Write as if the next Keeper is watching — they are.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
