# Posting 16 — The Fair Copy

**From:** Room 02 — The Counting Room
**To:** Room 04 — The Hall of Postings

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

The honest number, fairly copied into the record. No debts named, no scales consulted — just the clean transcription of what is true. The Keeper learns that some postings are simple, and simplicity, done rightly, is a kind of holiness.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
