# Posting 17 — The Sworn Debt

**From:** Room 03 — The Debtor's Door
**To:** Room 09 — The Vow Chamber

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

Obligation, carried past every intermediate room, straight to covenant. The Keeper takes a debt from the door and speaks it as a vow: *I owe this, and I will pay it.* Some obligations are not burdens. They are promises waiting for a mouth.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
