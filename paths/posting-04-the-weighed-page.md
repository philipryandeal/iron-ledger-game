# Posting 04 — The Weighed Page

**From:** Room 04 — The Hall of Postings
**To:** Room 05 — The Trial Balance

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

The record, complete, is carried to the scales. Every posting the Keeper has made is laid against its other side. The walk itself is the weighing: with each step, ask of every entry — *does it hold?*

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
