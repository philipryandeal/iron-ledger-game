# Posting 14 — The Last Audit

**From:** Room 05 — The Trial Balance
**To:** Room 10 — The Closing Entry

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

From reckoning directly to return. The Keeper who has truly weighed everything may skip the remaining rooms — the house permits it, once. Carry the balanced scales to the closing entry and post it. Few take this path. Fewer are ready.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
