# Posting 19 — The Finished Work

**From:** Room 07 — The Forge Ledger
**To:** Room 10 — The Closing Entry

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

What was transformed is ready to be closed. The Keeper carries the reforged work from the forge to the final posting. The metal has cooled; the shape holds. Lay it down. It is finished, and it is good.
