# Posting 15 — The Kept Fire

**From:** Room 01 — The Widow's Pantry
**To:** Room 07 — The Forge Ledger

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk it slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

---

What is kept can be fuel. The Keeper carries something preserved from the pantry — a memory, a jar, a name — into the forge, and learns that keeping and burning are not opposites. Some things are kept *for* the fire.

---

*The books remember the crossing, even if the Keeper forgets.*
