# The Twenty-Two Postings

*Every path is a posting: debited from one chamber, credited to the next. Walk them slowly. Nothing here is scored.*

Nine postings climb the house in order, chamber to chamber. Thirteen cross between distant rooms — shortcuts, long mercies, and vows carried the whole length of the ledger. The house may be traveled up and down, back and forth.
