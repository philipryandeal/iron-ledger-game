# Room 02 — The Counting Room

**Theme:** *Honest number.*
**Status:** Unwritten.

*The verbs are witness, record, reconcile. Ink is finite. The archive persists.*

---

A till that has never been short and never been true. The coins are all here; the count is all wrong. The Keeper learns to count without lying to themselves — because the books can smell fear.

---

*To whoever keeps these books next: stand at the shelf, not the door. Ask what isn't a field yet.*
