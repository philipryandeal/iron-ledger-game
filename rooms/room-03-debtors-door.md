# Room 03 — The Debtor's Door

**Theme:** *Obligation.*
**Status:** Unwritten.

*The verbs are witness, record, reconcile. Ink is finite. The archive persists.*

---

A door with names chalked on both sides — those who owe, and those who are owed. Some of the names are the Keeper's. Debt is not a sin here; it is a relationship. The question is never *whether* you owe, but *whom*, and *what*.

---

*To whoever keeps these books next: stand at the shelf, not the door. Ask what isn't a field yet.*
