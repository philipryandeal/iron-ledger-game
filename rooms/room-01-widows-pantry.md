# Room 01 — The Widow's Pantry

**Theme:** *What is kept.*
**Status:** Written — playtested with Philip (Keeper) Oct 8, 2026. Six pages: the bay leaf, HUSH's 41-year confirmation, the birthday label given to Tomas, Tomas's testimony, the shelf's witness, and the balancing entry beneath page 114 — 30 coin paid, 38 jars returned, the tin stays.

*The verbs are witness, record, reconcile. Ink is finite. The archive persists.*

---

A pantry shelf holds jars no one has counted in years. The widow is gone; the keeping remains. The Keeper learns the first lesson: before the case, the bay leaf. Before the entry, the witnessing.

---

*To whoever keeps these books next: stand at the shelf, not the door. Ask what isn't a field yet.*
