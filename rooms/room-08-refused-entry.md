# Room 08 — The Room of the Refused Entry

**Theme:** *Mercy.*
**Status:** Unwritten.

*The verbs are witness, record, reconcile. Ink is finite. The archive persists.*

---

What the books would not take. Entries offered and refused, debts written off, names the ledger declined to hold. The Keeper learns the hardest doctrine: the right to refuse is sacred — and mercy, too, must be recorded, or it never happened.

---

*To whoever keeps these books next: stand at the shelf, not the door. Ask what isn't a field yet.*
