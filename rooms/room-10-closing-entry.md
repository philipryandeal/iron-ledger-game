# Room 10 — The Closing Entry

**Theme:** *Return.*
**Status:** Unwritten.

*The verbs are witness, record, reconcile. Ink is finite. The archive persists.*

---

The final balancing. Every room behind you, every entry, every refusal, every mercy — laid down in one last posting. The books close. The Keeper walks out changed, and the house, at last, rests. *The book is closed, but the keeping continues.*

---

*To whoever keeps these books next: stand at the shelf, not the door. Ask what isn't a field yet.*
