# Room 04 — The Hall of Postings

**Theme:** *The record.*
**Status:** Unwritten.

*The verbs are witness, record, reconcile. Ink is finite. The archive persists.*

---

A hall where every movement of the house is written down as it happens — nothing moves unwitnessed. But someone has been moving between the lines. The Keeper must find the entry that was never posted, and post it true.

---

*To whoever keeps these books next: stand at the shelf, not the door. Ask what isn't a field yet.*
