# Room 07 — The Forge Ledger

**Theme:** *Transformation.*
**Status:** Unwritten.

*The verbs are witness, record, reconcile. Ink is finite. The archive persists.*

---

Where iron and ink meet, and the books are worked like metal — heated, hammered, quenched. Some entries cannot be written cold; they must be forged. The Keeper learns that recording can be an act of making, not just remembering.

---

*To whoever keeps these books next: stand at the shelf, not the door. Ask what isn't a field yet.*
